Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Nextier Studio handles information you provide when using our website or contacting us about a project.

Last updated: October 27, 2026

1. Who We Are

Nextier Studio is a digital solutions studio providing services such as website development, ecommerce development, digital advertising, analytics, and website optimization.

For privacy-related questions, contact us using the email address published on our Contact page.

2. Information You Provide

When you submit a project inquiry, we may receive information such as:

  • Your name and email address.
  • Your business name or website URL, if provided.
  • Details about your project, service requirements, or budget.
  • Other information you choose to include in your message.

Please do not submit passwords, payment card details, or other sensitive information through our contact form.

3. How We Use Information

We may use inquiry information to:

  • Respond to your questions and project inquiries.
  • Understand your requirements and prepare proposals.
  • Communicate with you about a potential or ongoing project.
  • Protect the website and prevent misuse.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information to third parties. We do not use inquiry details for unrelated purposes without an appropriate basis.

4. Contact Forms and EmailJS

Our contact form uses EmailJS to transmit submitted inquiries to our configured email destination. Information entered into the form may be processed by EmailJS and relevant email service providers to deliver the message.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and service terms. Review the applicable provider documentation before publishing this policy.

5. Cookies and Analytics

This website may use essential technologies needed for its operation. If analytics, advertising pixels, or other tracking tools are enabled, they may collect technical information about website usage, subject to the applicable settings and consent requirements.

Before publishing, update this section to list the analytics, advertising, and cookie technologies actually enabled on the site. Remove references to tools that are not used.

6. Sharing and Service Providers

Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate the website, deliver communications, provide requested services, or comply with legal obligations.

We aim to limit sharing to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

7. Data Retention and Security

We retain inquiry information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to requests, manage business relationships, maintain appropriate records, and meet legal obligations.

No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. We take reasonable steps to protect information but cannot guarantee absolute security.

Before publication, define a practical retention period and follow it consistently.

8. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to object to certain processing.

To make a privacy request, contact us using the email address listed on our Contact page. We may need to verify your request before acting on it.

9. Third-Party Websites

Our website may link to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Review their policies before submitting information to them.

10. Children’s Privacy

Our services are intended for businesses and people seeking professional digital services. Do not submit children's personal information through this website.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when our services, website technologies, or legal obligations change. The latest revision date will be displayed at the top of this page.

Questions about your privacy?

If you have a question about this policy or a privacy-related request, please contact us.
